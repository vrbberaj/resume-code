STAGE 1 • 15-MINUTE TELEPHONIC / ZOOM SCREENING

1st Call: Dean & HOD Screening Master Guide

This exploratory conversation is a Mutual Fit & Reality Check. The Dean or Faculty Chair wants to confirm your motivation to transition from corporate tech, identify which studio courses you can lead, and verify joining feasibility.

The 5 Core Screening Questions & Speaking Scripts
1

"After 18+ years leading tech & design at IBM, Zebra, Ericsson, and Samsung, why do you want a full-time Professor of Practice role now?"

Dean's Intent: Testing genuine passion for full-time teaching vs. looking for a casual retirement role.
"Over the last 18 years, I have built and scaled design systems, UX architectures, and digital products impacting millions of users across global enterprises like IBM, Zebra, Ericsson, and Samsung. Throughout this journey, the most rewarding part of my career has always been mentorship—training engineers and designers to think systemically and bridge design vision with code.

Having taught engineering cohorts previously under Anna University, academia has always been close to my heart. At this stage of my career, I want to institutionalize my industry learnings full-time—directing studio courses, building industry-grade design labs, and preparing the next generation of graduates to be day-one industry ready."
Keywords to Land: Institutionalizing industry practice, studio direction, student mentorship.
Tone to Convey: High energy, dedicated educator, committed to a full-time campus presence.
2

"Looking at our B.Des / M.Des curriculum, which specific courses or studios can you immediately lead?"

Dean's Intent: Checking if your industry skills map directly to university syllabus codes and studio tracks.
"I can immediately lead core studio and theory courses across three major tracks:
1. Interaction Design & HCI: Information architecture, cognitive workflow optimization, usability heuristics, and user research methodologies.
2. Scalable Design Systems: Multi-brand design tokens, UI component libraries, and the design-to-engineering translation pipeline.
3. Live Industry Capstones & Studio Jury: Mentoring 3rd and 4th-year graduation thesis projects using production-grade sprint models, client critique, and portfolio defense."
Studio Pedagogy: Contrast textbook design with real-world enterprise delivery constraints.
Cross-Discipline Fit: Highlight ability to bridge Design and Computer Science students.
3

"How do you view teaching and research as a non-PhD Professor of Practice?"

Dean's Intent: Verifying that you understand your distinct role alongside traditional PhD academic faculty.
"The UGC created the Professor of Practice framework specifically for industry leaders like me—bringing 18+ years of high-stakes, real-world execution that cannot be taught from a textbook.

While traditional PhD faculty focus on fundamental academic theory, my value is in practice-led pedagogy: teaching how enterprise design systems actually function at scale, how to navigate real-world trade-offs, and how to build production-ready digital products. Furthermore, having taught engineering subjects previously under Anna University, I am already comfortable with lesson planning, grading rubrics, and formal curriculum structures."
UGC Mandate: Point to UGC PoP guidelines validating 15+ years of distinguished practice.
Pedagogical Ease: Mention your prior Anna University teaching comfort.
4

"How will you leverage your corporate network to benefit our students and institution?"

Dean's Intent: Every design school wants a PoP who improves student internships, placements, and industry jury panels.
"Having spent 18+ years leading teams across Bangalore, Chennai, and global product hubs, I bring a strong network across enterprise software, telecom, healthcare, and IoT.

I plan to leverage this network in three concrete ways:
• Live Industry Briefs: Sourcing real-world problem statements from active companies for 3rd-year studio sprints.
• Industry Jury Panels: Inviting design directors and technical leads from top tech firms for mid-term and final project critiques.
• Internship & Hiring Pipeline: Creating direct recruitment pathways for our graduating design cohorts."
Tangible Value: Placement boosts, external industry jury, sponsored capstones.
Ecosystem Impact: Raising the school's visibility with tier-1 tech employers.
5

"What are your practical constraints regarding joining timeline and compensation?"

Dean's Intent: Budget alignment and semester calendar readiness.
"I am committed to a dedicated, full-time institutional appointment. On timeline, I am flexible to align with your upcoming semester schedule or join on standard 30-day notice.

On compensation, I understand the university’s consolidated Professor-grade norms under the UGC Professor of Practice framework and am happy to align with standard institutional benchmarks for senior practice faculty."
Flexibility: Ready for full-time residential campus schedule.
Compensation Stance: Professional, aligned with UGC/HEI consolidated Professor scale.

2 High-Power Questions YOU Must Ask the Dean

When the Dean asks "Do you have any questions for us?", asking these shows strategic vision and flips the dynamic from being evaluated to peer alignment:

Question A (Curriculum & AI):
"How is the School of Design currently approaching the integration of AI tools and tokenized Design Systems into your 3rd and 4th-year studio curriculum?"
Question B (Industry Capstones):
"What are the department's primary goals for industry-sponsored capstone projects over the coming academic year, and how do you envision a Professor of Practice driving that?"
