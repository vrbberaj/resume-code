01 • Business Context & Stakes

The Enterprise Crisis: Why Legacy Processes Were Failing

With 14 distributed software squads building products across cloud, data, and security, visual drift was spiraling out of control. Developers hardcoded hex values, copied CSS overrides from Stack Overflow, and repeatedly re-implemented button variations. Sprint handoffs were fraught with friction, resulting in over 70% of front-end QA tickets being visual regression bugs and breaking corporate WCAG accessibility compliance.

Strategic Design Leadership Mandate
Establish a seamless DesignOps token synchronizer connecting Figma Variables directly to React and Web Components repositories via automated CI/CD pipelines, governed by a democratic Contribution Council.
02 • In-Situ Field Research & Mental Models

Contextual Inquiries & Empathy Mapping

Conducting interviews with senior engineering squad leads and designers across Bangalore, Austin, and San Jose, we found that engineers were eager to adopt design systems, but had no automated bridge. Manually updating hex codes whenever brand guidelines shifted was error-prone and wasted 25% of front-end sprint capacity.

03 • Information Architecture & Process Engineering

Before & After Workflow Transformation

07 • Hard Business ROI & Leadership CAR Defense

Leadership Interview Defense Q&A (Situation, Action, Result)

Defense examining DesignOps leadership, federated governance models, and scaling design consistency across global engineering teams.

Q1 How did you scale design governance across 14 distributed squads without slowing down agile release trains?
Context / Situation

14 product squads were reinventing UI components, creating massive design debt, hardcoding colors, and failing corporate accessibility audits.

UX Leadership Action

I established the Carbon Token Engine and founded the DesignOps Contribution Council. We implemented a 3-tier token hierarchy (Global → Semantic → Component) and automated the sync: when a token updates in Figma, a GitHub Action auto-generates PRs into squad repos via Style-Dictionary.

Business Result

Front-end sprint velocity increased by 28%, visual regression defects fell by 70%, and light/dark theme parity was achieved automatically across all 14 squads.
