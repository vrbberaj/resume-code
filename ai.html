Part 01 • UX for AI (Product Architecture)

Eliminating “verification fatigue” in mission-critical software

In systems engineering, cybersecurity, banking, and fleet operations, mistakes are expensive. When AI acts like a black box, checking its output takes longer than doing the work manually—so operators turn it off and compliance blocks it.

Why Generic AI Fails

Black-Box Chat & Silent Mutations

  • Applies changes to models, policies, or fleets without showing what changed or why.
  • Displays raw percentages (“92% confident”) without explaining what evidence is missing.
  • Forces operators to audit every single line manually—creating severe verification fatigue.
  • Blocked by enterprise CISO and compliance teams due to zero auditability or rollback.
How My Teams Design AI

Visible Reasoning, Calibrated Control & Diffs

  • Shows a clear step-by-step reasoning trace linked directly to source requirements or telemetry.
  • Translates model confidence into explicit interaction tiers—automating routine work while gating risk.
  • Presents side-by-side before/after diffs with blast-radius previews and one-click human approval.
  • Logs every AI suggestion and human sign-off for zero-violation enterprise compliance.

Quality Gate • Design Governance

My 10-point enterprise AI UX rubric

Before any AI feature moved from design into engineering sprints at IBM, squads scored the flow against these ten checks so every product shared one consistent trust bar:

01

Source Provenance

Every AI output links directly to the exact requirement, log, or policy clause that produced it.

02

Visible Reasoning Trace

Users can inspect why the AI chose an action in plain domain language—never opaque model jargon.

03

Blast-Radius Preview

Before an agent acts, the UI shows exactly how many devices, users, or downstream nodes are affected.

04

Actionable Confidence

Confidence scores map to clear next actions (auto, review diff, or manual), never standalone numbers.

05

One-Click Reversibility

Automated and approved agent actions include a clear undo or staged-canary rollback path.

06

Graceful Manual Fallback

When AI fails or confidence drops, the user lands in a pre-populated manual flow—never a dead end.

07

Faster Than Manual Work

Reviewing and approving an AI diff must measurably beat doing the task by hand (zero verification fatigue).

08

Honest Multi-Step Latency

Long-running agent workflows show live step progress and allow safe cancellation mid-run.

09

Immutable Compliance Log

Distinguishes AI-generated drafts from human-approved changes for regulatory and CISO audits.

10

In-Flow Signal Capture

Captures operator edits and rejections naturally in the workflow—replacing noisy thumbs-up/down popups.

Part 02 • AI for UX (Team Velocity & DesignOps)

How I bring AI into the team’s own design workflow

Individual AI speed only matters if an entire team can use it safely. Across a 14-person group spanning UI/UX design, UX research, and content, I standardized how we synthesize research, stress-test edge cases, and prototype in live code.

LIVE WORKFLOW WALKTHROUGH
Phase 1 · Privacy-Safe Discovery Synthesis

Redacted Enterprise Logs → Exhaustive Failure Matrix

We never paste raw customer PII into external models. Using redacted field transcripts and domain SLA rules inside enterprise-governed LLMs, we surface rare failure states before touching Figma.

[Input] 18 anonymized operator transcripts + SLA compliance rules
[Output] 14 non-happy-path states mapped (stale telemetry, partial rollback, permission conflict)
[Impact] Discovery synthesis compressed from 8 days → 3 hours
Phase 2 · Interactive Code Prototyping

Static Flows → Live Token-Constrained HTML/React Sandbox

Instead of debating flat wireframes, we generate interactive code prototypes strictly bound to existing Design System tokens—testing real keyboard density, latency skeletons, and human override diffs on Day 2.

[Constraint] Carbon v11 semantic tokens + WCAG 2.2 AA contrast locked
[Prototype] Live interactive blast-radius diff & bulk keyboard triage
[Impact] Validated with Principal Engineers in Sprint Zero; UI rework cut by 70%
Phase 3 · 14-Person Team Multiplier

Personal Workflow → Repeatable Team Playbook

Codified shared prompt libraries, PII redaction rules, content-linting checks, and our 10-point AI UX rubric across UI/UX designers, UX researchers, and content specialists.

[Governance] Zero raw customer PII in external models • Mandatory human critique
[Adoption] 14 specialists upskilled via weekly AI-UX studio pairing
[Outcome] End-to-end feature cycle time cut from 6 weeks → under 2 weeks
Stack: IBM watsonx • Copilot • ChatGPT Enterprise • NotebookLM 6 wks → <2 wks cycle

1. Faster Research Without Privacy Risk

Strict PII redaction first—then AI synthesis across interview transcripts and support tickets to uncover edge cases early.

2. Live Prototypes Over Static Deck Debates

Putting working, token-compliant interactions in front of users and engineers in days so we agree on behavior before sprint commitment.

3. AI Assists Craft — Humans Own Decisions

AI accelerates first-pass synthesis, microcopy checks, and state coverage, while designers and researchers own problem framing and user judgment.
