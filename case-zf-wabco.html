Q1 How did you balance rich real-time telematics with the life-or-death ergonomics of commercial truck driving?
Context / Situation Commercial fleet operators wanted dozens of data streams (CAN-bus engine torque, tire pressure, tachograph hours, dispatch messages) visible to drivers, creating severe cognitive overload.
UX Leadership Action I instituted a rigorous 3-zone visual glance hierarchy aligned with EU automotive safety standards. Critical driving data remained under 0.8s glance time, navigation cues stayed under 1.2s, and all non-critical fleet telematics were routed exclusively to the dispatcher web console.
Business Result Successfully deployed across 80,000+ heavy trucks in Europe with 100% regulatory compliance and an 18% reduction in route deviations.
