Q1 How did you design for user trust when introducing autonomous AI agents into high-stakes enterprise systems?
Context / Situation Enterprise compliance officers rejected autonomous AI execution in SAP and Salesforce due to fear of hallucinated data corrupting corporate books.
UX Leadership Action I designed the 3-Tier Confidence Gate Matrix. Every agentic output is assigned an explainable score. Scores ≥90% execute autonomously; 70–89% present a side-by-side diff drawer where humans approve changes with one click; scores <70% fall back to manual entry with clear attribution.
Business Result Reduced enterprise task completion time by 62% while maintaining 100% compliance across 14 distributed development squads.
