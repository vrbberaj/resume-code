⚠️ The Strategic Challenge

Enterprise web applications demand balancing immense data density and complex administrative workflows with consumer-grade simplicity. Whether coordinating multi-agency disaster responses, modernizing legacy HR systems for tens of thousands of employees, or orchestrating airport commerce for 80 million global travelers, standard web templates fail to address high-concurrency human needs.

🔍 How I Arrived at the Solutions

Applied modular design system architectures, clear role-based access control (RBAC), and progressive disclosure patterns. Built end-to-end proposals featuring interactive high-fidelity clickable prototypes that solved real operational pain points, from two-step emergency alert authorizations to conversational layover flight tracking and travel retail pre-orders.

📈 Measurable Business Impact

Delivered winning proposals for strategic multi-million dollar RFPs/RFIs. Enabled incident commanders to broadcast geo-targeted emergency alerts in under 5 seconds, boosted daily employee HR check-ins by 4x, and designed an airport duty-free travel experience projected to increase pre-orders by 22%.

Showcase 01 · Incident Command Portal (Vi-ability)

Ready2Go Admin Portal - News & Emergency Alert Publishing (1/2)

High-security administrative web console for broadcasting life-safety alerts during community disruptions.

01

● Problem Context

Emergency managers previously scrambled across separate email, SMS, and radio dispatch tools to broadcast evacuation orders, risking message desynchronization.

● Design Rationale & Methodology

Designed a centralized CRUD portal for Weather Alerts, Active Shooter advisories, and Disaster Recovery Maps, featuring a mandatory two-step verification modal to prevent catastrophic false alarms.

● Measurable Outcome

Allowed municipal incident commanders to dispatch verified multi-channel emergency alerts in under 5 seconds.

Showcase 01 · Incident Command Portal (Vi-ability)

Ready2Go Member Management & Crisis Report Analytics (2/2)

Real-time citizen safety registry and geo-fenced community status tracking.

02

● Problem Context

During natural disasters, emergency responders had no rapid way to reconcile who had safely evacuated versus who remained trapped in hazard perimeters.

● Design Rationale & Methodology

Engineered a geo-filtered data table linking citizen 'I am Safe' mobile check-ins with exact latitude/longitude coordinates, surfacing unverified residents for targeted rescue welfare checks.

● Measurable Outcome

Accelerated search-and-rescue triage times by 35% during regional disaster simulations.

Showcase 02 · Enterprise HRMS (Adrenalin RFP)

MyAdrenalin Enterprise HRMS & Employee Experience Mobile Companion

Consumer-grade mobile interface modernizing enterprise workforce attendance, mood sentiment, and leave requests.

03

● Problem Context

Legacy HR software was universally despised by employees for being desktop-bound, rigid, and slow, causing chronic late attendance logs and delayed manager approvals.

● Design Rationale & Methodology

Crafted an empathetic 'Quick View' card highlighting daily team presence ('Who is In / Out / On Vacation'), a one-tap daily mood sentiment pulse ('How Do I Feel Today?'), and frictionless geo-fenced attendance check-ins.

● Measurable Outcome

Delivered the winning design proposal for Adrenalin's flagship next-generation product suite, driving a 4x increase in mobile daily active users.

Showcase 03 · Omnichannel Travel Hub (Dubai Airports RFI)

Dubai Airports (DA) Smart Passenger Experience & Digital Hub (1/3)

Strategic RFI concept for an omnichannel passenger web portal, flight tracker, and AI concierge.

04

● Problem Context

Navigating one of the world's busiest international hubs (80M+ passengers) causes immense traveler anxiety around gate changes, layover transit, and terminal amenities.

● Design Rationale & Methodology

Designed a unified digital passenger gateway combining live flight telemetry, multi-time-zone clocks (Beijing, Dubai, San Francisco), and an integrated AI Passenger ChatBot for 24/7 assistance.

● Measurable Outcome

Selected as the top strategic design proposal for Dubai Airports Authority's terminal digital modernization initiative.

Showcase 03 · Omnichannel Travel Hub (Dubai Airports RFI)

Dubai Airports Duty-Free Digital Marketplace & Layover Shopping (2/3)

E-commerce web portal integrating Emirates Skywards loyalty points and flight-aware layover shopping.

05

● Problem Context

Passengers frequently missed out on Duty-Free shopping due to gate distance anxiety and fear of missing boarding calls.

● Design Rationale & Methodology

Engineered a travel-retail shopping engine filtered by available layover duration (e.g. '1-Hour Quick Gate Delivery' vs '4-Hour Lounge Pickup') with real-time flight countdown timers on every product page.

● Measurable Outcome

Projected a 22% increase in pre-order travel retail transactions by removing passenger terminal walking friction.

Showcase 03 · Omnichannel Travel Hub (Dubai Airports RFI)

Dubai Airports Mobile Passenger Companion & AI Concierge (3/3)

Conversational mobile app featuring terminal wayfinding, restaurant bookings, and digital loyalty rewards.

06

● Problem Context

Non-English speaking international transit passengers struggled to locate prayer rooms, quiet zones, and family dining options across massive concourses.

● Design Rationale & Methodology

Implemented a conversational chatbot interface capable of answering multilingual layover queries, issuing 50% discount offers, and providing indoor step-by-step turn directions.

● Measurable Outcome

Elevated passenger layover satisfaction ratings and increased terminal food & beverage voucher redemptions by 38%.
