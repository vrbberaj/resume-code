Telecom CPQ & Financial Modeling Engine • Ericsson Global

Sales Automation Tool

A real-time financial simulation engine and CPQ portal replacing a fragile, 14-tab manual Excel workbook with keyboard-first scenario modeling for multi-million MSEK telecom infrastructure deals.

Client & Domain
Ericsson Global · Telecom CPQ
Role & Ownership
Lead UX Architect · End-to-End
Core Methodologies
Workflow Deconstruction, Reactive Modeling, RACI Governance
Business Impact
Bid Turnaround: 14 Days → 4 Hours

⚠️ The Operational Bottleneck

Multi-million MSEK telecom infrastructure deals (5G rollouts, RAN, Core Networks) required complex financial pricing across Sales Directors, Bid Architects, and Commercial Finance heads in 45 countries. Calculations lived inside a 20MB, 14-tab macro-heavy Excel workbook. Generating deal options took 10 to 14 days, with frequent formula corruption and catastrophic margin miscalculations.

🔍 How I Arrived at the Solution

Conducted contextual inquiry and shadowing sessions with global deal bid managers in Sweden, UK, and India. Deconstructed 14 disconnected spreadsheet tabs into 4 clear executive views: Deal Pipeline Triage, Side-by-Side Scenario Simulation (Scenario A vs B), Dynamic Headcount Allocation, and an Ericsson vs Operator RACI matrix that protected contract margins.

📈 Measurable Business Impact

Slashed deal modeling turnaround from 14 days to 4 hours. Eliminated formula corruption risks across live bids, protected an estimated 4.2% in contract margin through rigorous RACI clarity, and achieved 100% adoption across 45 national sales operating units.

Phase 01 · Pipeline Visibility & Opportunity Triage

Deal Opportunity Pipeline & Commercial Margin Overview

Centralized executive cockpit providing real-time visibility into active multi-million MSEK telecom deals across global regions.

01

● Problem Context

Commercial finance directors previously lacked a unified view of active pipeline bids, relying on fragmented email attachments that made workload balancing and revenue forecasting near-impossible.

● Design Rationale & Methodology

Engineered a high-density, scannable data grid with instant probability weighting, currency normalization (MSEK), and live margin risk flags to prioritize urgent deal reviews at a glance.

● Measurable Outcome

Reduced weekly executive bid review meetings by 60% and unified deal reporting across 45 regional operating companies.

Phase 02 · Reactive Financial Scenario Engine

Side-by-Side Financial Scenario Simulation (Scenario A vs Scenario B)

Interactive modeling canvas allowing sales leaders to test staffing, risk provisions, and margin thresholds side-by-side in real time.

02

● Problem Context

Comparing deal variations (e.g. aggressive pricing vs conservative risk buffers) previously required manually duplicating massive Excel workbooks, often leading to stale numbers and formula drift.

● Design Rationale & Methodology

Designed a two-column comparative architecture with synchronized reactive sliders. Modifying transition costs, engineering headcounts, or risk buffers instantly updates bottom-line EBITA and 5-year cash-flow curves.

● Measurable Outcome

Allowed executive deal teams to simulate complex pricing trade-offs in 15 minutes during live negotiations, eliminating days of spreadsheet recalculation.

Phase 03 · Operational Feasibility & Governance

Dynamic Resource Allocation & Ericsson vs Operator RACI Matrix

Operational handoff framework linking domain specialist headcount directly to contractual boundary definitions.

03

● Problem Context

Post-contract margin erosion frequently happened because operational boundaries between Ericsson engineers and telecom operator personnel were ambiguously defined in sales proposals.

● Design Rationale & Methodology

Synthesized an interactive RACI governance matrix directly into the pricing flow, tying onsite/offshore headcount sliders directly to contractual deliverables and preventing unbilled scope creep.

● Measurable Outcome

Eliminated scope disputes post-contract signing, protecting an average of 4.2% in net contract margin across tier-1 telecom operator contracts.
