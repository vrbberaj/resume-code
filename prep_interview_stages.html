Stage 2

15-Minute Studio Masterclass / Teaching Demonstration

Universities want to see how you command a classroom. Rather than an abstract theoretical lecture, your winning formula is a Practice-Driven Studio Masterclass.

Recommended Lecture Title:

"Bridging HCI, Enterprise Design Systems, and Scalable Code: From Concept to Production"

Slide 1 • 2 Mins

The Industry Reality Gap

Why 80% of student design portfolios fail in enterprise engineering—the disconnect between Figma mocks and multi-device production constraints.

Slide 2 • 3 Mins

The Design Token Architecture

Explaining design tokens (color, typography, elevation, spacing) as the contract between designers and frontend developers across iOS, Android, and Web.

Slide 3 • 4 Mins

Live Heuristic Studio Critique

Demonstrating how to evaluate a complex enterprise workflow (e.g. healthcare or industrial barcode scanner) against Nielsen's 10 Heuristics.

Slide 4 • 3 Mins

Design-to-Code Pipeline

How modern UI frameworks consume design tokens using component libraries, CI/CD design audits, and automated accessibility checks.

Slide 5 • 2 Mins

Student Capstone Brief

Presenting a sample 4-week studio brief: students design a mission-critical dashboard, build token architecture, and test with live users.

Slide 6 • 1 Min

Learning Outcomes & Takeaways

Bridging design thinking with engineering execution—producing graduates ready for day-one product roles.

Masterclass Pro-Tip: Spend 2 minutes during the lecture asking the panel (acting as your "students") an interactive question: "If our client demands 3 enterprise themes, do we create 3 separate Figma files or 1 tokenized system?" This immediately proves your dynamic classroom presence!
Stage 3

Professional Portfolio & Enterprise Case Defense

The selection committee will review your 18-year career across IBM, Zebra Technologies, Ericsson, Samsung, and Infosys. Focus on systemic scale and leadership, not just visual artifacts.

Pillar 1

Zebra Technologies Scale

Architecting rugged industrial interfaces and barcode-scanning UX for warehouse logistics operating in sub-zero and hazardous environments.

Pillar 2

Samsung Healthcare UX

Driving global usability research and medical interface design that earned a 10-Year US Business Visa recognition for high-impact innovation.

Pillar 3

Ericsson Telecom Systems

Leading mission-critical network monitoring dashboards, data visualization, and receiving the Spot Award for sustaining innovation culture.

Pillar 4

IBM Enterprise Architecture

Bridging multi-disciplinary engineering and UX teams across high-volume enterprise transformation accounts.

Portfolio Presentation Rule: Always frame your projects using the CAR Framework: Context (Scale & Users) $\rightarrow$ Architecture & Strategy (Your Design System / HCI Decision) $\rightarrow$ Results & Translation (How you teach this to students).
Stage 4

Executive Board & Vice-Chancellor Panel Defense

The final round with the Vice-Chancellor, Registrar, and External Experts centers on institutional vision, national education alignment, and department growth.

Q: "How will your teaching align with NEP 2020 (National Education Policy) multidisciplinary guidelines?"
"NEP 2020 explicitly mandates breaking down silos between engineering and creative arts. With my B.E. in IT and 18+ years of design leadership, I am uniquely positioned to run cross-faculty electives—bringing Computer Science students into design studios and Design students into frontend architectures."
Q: "How do you evaluate and grade student design work objectively without bias?"
"I implement structured industry rubrics: 25% User Research Depth, 25% Heuristic & Accessibility Compliance, 25% Technical Feasibility & Design Tokens, and 25% Presentation & Portfolio Defense. This removes subjective 'aesthetic bias' and grades students on industry competence."
Q: "What is your 3-year vision for our Department of Design?"
"Year 1: Establish an enterprise-standard design token studio and integrate live industry capstones.
Year 2: Launch an Industry Advisory Jury and establish funded student internship pipelines with top tech employers.
Year 3: Position the institution as a national benchmark for applied HCI and design-to-engineering innovation."
