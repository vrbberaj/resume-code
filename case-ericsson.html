01 • Business Context & Stakes

The Enterprise Crisis: Why Legacy Processes Were Failing

At Ericsson Global, over 1,200 technical sales engineers across 45 countries were quoting complex 5G network infrastructure contracts worth upwards of $450 million annually. However, the entire commercial machinery relied on a monolithic, unversioned 50MB Microsoft Excel workbook containing 14 dense tabs, fragile Visual Basic macros, and manual regional rate cards.

Whenever a sales engineer modified a discount cell, broken #REF! formulas frequently cascaded across interdependent hardware calculations. Worse still, unauthorized discounting slipped through unnoticed, costing Ericsson an estimated $18M+ annually in margin erosion. Quotes crawled through a 6-day email bottleneck waiting for Commercial Directors to manually verify Excel cell formulas.

Strategic Design Leadership Mandate
Architect an enterprise CPQ cloud application that eliminated spreadsheet calculation errors and enforced automated gross margin guardrails, while strictly matching the keyboard data-entry velocity that senior electrical engineers demanded.
02 • In-Situ Field Research & Mental Models

07 • Hard Business ROI & Executive CAR Defense

Executive Interview Defense Q&A (Situation, Action, Result)

Direct leadership defenses addressing critical business tensions between sales velocity and financial compliance.

Q1 Sales engineers are notoriously protective of their Excel spreadsheets. How did you overcome their intense resistance to a new web tool?
Context / Situation

Previous corporate attempts to replace the 14-tab Excel sheet with heavy web wizards failed with under 15% adoption because sales engineers felt slow, handcuffed, and restricted by multi-step dropdown forms.

UX Leadership Action

During contextual inquiry in Stockholm, I discovered engineers loved Excel not for its aesthetics, but for keyboard velocity. I scrapped the wizard paradigm and architected a high-speed data grid featuring arrow-key cell navigation, tabular copy-paste, and inline search. We preserved their muscle memory while silently moving the catalog and pricing validation to the cloud.

Business Result

Adoption exceeded 94% within the first month. Quote turnaround dropped from 6 days to 42 minutes, and the project won 1st Place in the Ericsson Global Hack-fest Optimization Contest.

Q2 What was the biggest tension between Sales and Finance, and how did your UX architecture resolve it?
Context / Situation

Sales demanded total pricing flexibility to close competitive telecom deals quickly, while Corporate Finance wanted strict lockouts to stop inadvertent discounting and margin erosion.

UX Leadership Action

I designed dynamic gross margin guardrails. If a discount kept margin ≥35%, the quote remained in the Green Channel for instant export. If it dipped into 28–34%, the UI triggered an inline escalation simulation showing projected revenue impact. If below 28%, the system locked the discount and prompted required Commercial Director approval.

Business Result

Eliminated subjective email arguments between Sales and Finance, protected over $18M in margin leakage across $450M in annual deals, and eliminated 100% of broken #REF! calculation errors.
