How I lead

Four things I believe about leading design

Leadership, hiring & delegation →

Clarity before pixels

Every project starts with a one-page brief: problem, user, success metric, constraints. If we can’t write it down, we’re not ready to design.

Delegate outcomes, not tasks

I give designers a problem and a definition of done, not a list of screens. I stay close through critique, not control.

Engineering is a design partner

Designers pair with engineers from sprint zero. Shared tokens, shared demos, shared credit.

Evidence over opinion

Research, usability scores and analytics settle debates, not seniority. That includes mine.
