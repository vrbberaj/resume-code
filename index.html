Q1 Sales engineers are notoriously protective of their Excel spreadsheets. How did you overcome their intense resistance to a new web tool?
Context / Situation Previous corporate attempts to replace the 14-tab Excel sheet with heavy web wizards failed with under 15% adoption because sales engineers felt slow, handcuffed, and restricted by multi-step dropdown forms.

Business Result Adoption exceeded 94% within the first month. Quote turnaround dropped from 6 days to 42 minutes, and the project won 1st Place in the Ericsson Global Hack-fest Optimization Contest.
Q2 What was the biggest tension between Sales and Finance, and how did your UX architecture resolve it?
Context / Situation Sales demanded total pricing flexibility to close competitive telecom deals quickly, while Corporate Finance wanted strict lockouts to stop inadvertent discounting and margin erosion.
UX Leadership Action I designed dynamic gross margin guardrails. If a discount kept margin ≥35%, the quote remained in the Green Channel for instant export. If it dipped into 28–34%, the UI triggered an inline escalation simulation showing projected revenue impact. If below 28%, the system locked the discount and prompted required Commercial Director approval.
Business Result Eliminated subjective email arguments between Sales and Finance, protected over $18M in margin leakage across $450M in annual deals, and eliminated 100% of broken #REF! calculation errors.
