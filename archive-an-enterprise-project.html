IoT Fleet Intelligence & Predictive Device Health • Zebra Technologies

An Enterprise Project

VisibilityIQ Foresight & Device Health platform engineered for tier-1 enterprise fleets (including Australia Post and Bed Bath & Beyond), tracking battery degradation and resolving application crashes across tens of thousands of frontline handhelds.

Client & Customers
Zebra Technologies · Australia Post, Bed Bath & Beyond
Role & Ownership
Principal UX Architect · Fleet Telemetry
Core Methodologies
Card Sorting, Telemetry IA, CEM Field Research, Root-Cause Diagnostics
Customer ROI
Saved $450K+ in Battery Replacements

⚠️ The Operational Bottleneck

Australia Post deployed tens of thousands of rugged mobile handhelds to mail carriers nationwide. Handhelds were shutting down dead by 11:00 AM on delivery routes, causing severe logistics delays. IT fleet managers assumed lithium-ion batteries had reached end-of-life and prepared to spend millions replacing entire battery inventories. However, IT lacked visibility into whether the drain was caused by battery cycle decay or unoptimized software.

🔍 How I Arrived at the Solution

Created the 'Jamie' IT Fleet Manager persona and conducted extensive multi-stakeholder card sorting to group 60+ raw IoT metrics. Structured an end-to-end Information Architecture tree from enterprise macro health down to single-battery serial diagnostics. Collaborated directly in Customer Experience Meetings (CEM) with Zebra Product Director Harry Banias and Australia Post Head of Operations Stephen Evans, unearthing that an unoptimized background APK was causing 100% CPU lockups.

📈 Measurable Business Impact

Proved that 80% of battery drain was caused by background software freezes (ANRs) rather than hardware degradation, saving Australia Post over $450,000 in unnecessary battery replacements. Recognized with official customer commendations from Australia Post and Viva Acqua.

Phase 01 · User Modeling

Enterprise Persona Archetype: Jamie - IT Fleet Manager

Behavioral profile, operational friction, and daily tooling needs of IT leaders overseeing 10,000+ mobile endpoints.

01

● Problem Context

Fleet managers were inundated with thousands of device alerts across regional hubs with no ability to separate urgent hardware failures from routine charge cycles.

● Design Rationale & Methodology

Mapped Jamie's complete daily journey from shift start triage to RMA repair logging, defining the essential telemetry widgets needed for high-confidence fleet oversight.

● Measurable Outcome

Anchored all dashboard design decisions around Jamie's core metric: ensuring 99.5% device availability during frontline shift delivery hours.

Phase 02 · Taxonomy Structuring

Multi-Stakeholder Open & Closed Card Sorting Workshop

Synthesizing 60+ raw IoT telemetry data points into intuitive operational clusters.

02

● Problem Context

Raw hardware telemetry (impedance, milliamp hours, ANRs, Wi-Fi signal, drop events) was presented in an engineering dump that confused operations managers.

● Design Rationale & Methodology

Conducted collaborative card-sorting workshops with IT managers and support engineers, clustering raw metrics into 4 intuitive categories: Device Health, Battery Life, Application Stability, and Repair Logistics.

● Measurable Outcome

Established the universal navigation taxonomy for Zebra's VisibilityIQ cloud suite, reducing menu traversal time by 52%.

Phase 02 · Information Architecture

Master Information Architecture & Telemetry Tree

End-to-end navigational hierarchy connecting global fleet health scores to single-serial diagnostics.

03

● Problem Context

Users needed to effortlessly navigate from a high-level fleet health score of '87' down to the specific serial number of a degraded battery in Melbourne.

● Design Rationale & Methodology

Architected a progressive 4-level drill-down path: Global Macro Fleet Health → Regional Facility Hubs → Device Model Cohorts (TC57/TC77) → Individual Serial Diagnostics.

● Measurable Outcome

Created a unified mental model that allowed support engineers to pinpoint faulty devices in under 3 clicks.

Phase 03 · Layout Prototyping

Mid-Fidelity Wireframes & Responsive Layout Explorations

Testing data density, tabular facet filtering, and glanceable status cards across screen sizes.

04

● Problem Context

Displaying 10,000+ devices with 15 telemetry attributes created extreme horizontal scrolling fatigue on standard 1080p monitors.

● Design Rationale & Methodology

Iterated through mid-fi layouts to develop a smart collapsible facet bar on the left paired with a pinned primary metric grid on the right, keeping critical thresholds visible at all times.

● Measurable Outcome

Eliminated horizontal scrolling on 98% of target enterprise screens while maintaining full diagnostic depth.

Phase 04 · Field Co-Creation

Customer Experience Meeting (CEM) Field Research Notes

Collaborative field session with Harry Banias (Zebra Product Director) and Stephen Evans (Australia Post Operations).

05

● Problem Context

Postal workers were reporting dead batteries by 11am, and executive management demanded immediate answers before approving multimillion-dollar battery purchase orders.

● Design Rationale & Methodology

Facilitated live dashboard validation sessions with customer stakeholders, comparing real-time battery voltage discharge curves against background application CPU spikes.

● Measurable Outcome

Discovered that battery discharge was not uniform: devices running a specific package version suffered severe CPU throttling, proving software—not hardware—was the culprit.

Phase 05 · Production UI Execution

Predictive Battery Health & Lifecycle Management Dashboard

Production interface highlighting charge cycles, degradation curves, and proactive replacement thresholds.

06

● Problem Context

Firms previously replaced batteries on an arbitrary 2-year calendar schedule, throwing away thousands of healthy batteries while keeping failing units in circulation.

● Design Rationale & Methodology

Engineered a predictive degradation curve categorizing batteries into 'Healthy (>80%)', 'Approaching End of Life (70-80%)', and 'Immediate Replacement (<70%)' based on real chemical telemetry.

● Measurable Outcome

Australia Post avoided replacing 18,000 viable batteries, generating an immediate operational savings of over $450,000.

Phase 05 · Root-Cause Diagnostics

Application Performance (ANR) & Crash Intelligence Dashboard

Deep-dive analytics correlating software unresponsiveness with device battery decay and worker downtime.

07

● Problem Context

App development teams blamed hardware battery decay for worker downtime, while hardware teams blamed unoptimized software.

● Design Rationale & Methodology

Designed an ANR (Application Not Responding) crash heatmap correlating app freeze events directly with battery temperature and discharge rates.

● Measurable Outcome

Provided the definitive empirical evidence that an unoptimized background sync APK was causing 80% of battery failures, leading to a quick software patch that restored full-day battery life.

Phase 05 · Customer Validation

Executive Validation Quotes & Customer ROI Endorsements

Direct customer feedback from Stephen Evans (Australia Post) and Maria Maestre (Viva Acqua).

08

● Problem Context

Enterprise software adoption fails if frontline operational managers find the interface unintuitive or disconnected from daily reality.

● Design Rationale & Methodology

Conducted post-rollout satisfaction reviews and documented executive customer endorsements on operational clarity, predictive accuracy, and time savings.

● Measurable Outcome

Stephen Evans commended the platform for transforming Australia Post's device operations, cementing VisibilityIQ as Zebra's flagship SaaS offering.
