⚠️ The Mobile Design Challenge

Mobile UX requires ruthless focus on glanceability, one-handed thumb ergonomics, and zero-latency performance. Whether engineering live cricket scorecards for tens of millions of simultaneous viewers, designing interfaces for elderly patients with low dexterity, or assisting children on the autism spectrum, a generic mobile design language leads to user abandonment and cognitive failure.

🔍 How I Arrived at the Solutions

Applied human-centered ergonomic principles tailored to specific user capabilities: oversized touch targets and high-contrast vitals for seniors, sensory-calm visual routines for neurodiverse children, and sub-second tabular cards with pixel-perfect engineering redlines for live sports streaming.

📈 Measurable Business Impact

Handled tens of millions of concurrent live sports fans during peak World Cup matches without UI lag, boosted elderly medication adherence to 91%, eliminated design-engineer handover rework through comprehensive redlines, and helped self-insured employers save thousands per medical procedure.
