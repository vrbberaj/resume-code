01 • Business Context & Stakes

The Enterprise Crisis: Why Legacy Processes Were Failing

Zebra Technologies powers the physical logistics of e-commerce giants. When a fulfillment center receives a shipment of 1,000 new rugged handheld scanners, IT technicians historically spent 4.5 business days individually tethering each device over USB to flash Wi-Fi certificates, OS updates, and inventory APKs.

Furthermore, once deployed onto the warehouse floor, unpredicted battery degradation mid-shift caused conveyor belt packaging lines to halt, stalling packing throughput and creating critical shift bottlenecks during peak fulfillment surges.

Strategic Design Leadership Mandate
Architect a cloud telematics console paired with an instant zero-touch hardware provisioning protocol, enabling 1,000 scanners to be staged in seconds without cables or manual touch interaction.
02 • In-Situ Field Research & Mental Models

Contextual Inquiries & Empathy Mapping

I spent three days on-site inside a -20°C cold-storage logistics distribution facility in Chicago. Shadowing pickers and IT leads, I observed that workers wore thick thermal gloves, rendering standard capacitive touchscreen gestures completely unusable. Condensation often blinded low-contrast displays, and workers relied entirely on physical trigger click feedback and audible beeps.

07 • Hard Business ROI & Leadership CAR Defense

Leadership Interview Defense Q&A (Situation, Action, Result)

Defense examining field-tested hardware UX, physical ergonomic constraints, and enterprise IoT telemetry.

Q1 How did your field research in cold-storage warehouses change your design approach for industrial hardware UX?
Context / Situation

Product management assumed standard capacitive touchscreen gestures would work on Android-based warehouse scanners. In reality, freezer workers wearing thermal gloves struggled with mis-taps and condensation.

UX Leadership Action

I spent 3 days shadowing operators inside a -20°C distribution center in Chicago. I redesigned the scanner UI around ultra-high contrast dark mode, oversized touch targets (min 64px), and hardware trigger mapping with haptic confirmation. We also designed the 1-Scan barcode protocol so operators never had to type Wi-Fi passwords.

Business Result

Staging time dropped from 4.5 days to 8 seconds per scanner. Fleet operational downtime dropped by 45%, and tier-1 support tickets decreased by 33% across 1.2M active devices.
