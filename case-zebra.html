Q1 How did your field research in cold-storage warehouses change your design approach for industrial hardware UX?
Context / Situation Product management assumed standard capacitive touchscreen gestures would work on Android-based warehouse scanners. In reality, freezer workers wearing thermal gloves struggled with mis-taps and condensation.
UX Leadership Action I spent 3 days shadowing operators inside a -20°C distribution center in Chicago. I redesigned the scanner UI around ultra-high contrast dark mode, oversized touch targets (min 64px), and hardware trigger mapping with haptic confirmation. We also designed the 1-Scan barcode protocol so operators never had to type Wi-Fi passwords.
Business Result Staging time dropped from 4.5 days to 8 seconds per scanner. Fleet operational downtime dropped by 45%, and tier-1 support tickets decreased by 33% across 1.2M active devices.
